We have finished our gamejam submission for the GMTK Jam 2026! It's a thrilling adventure experience where you have to escape from a building where everything has it's clock. People, animals and even the building. 

Description:

This Building is Rotten. Everybody's clock is ticking. Everything will burn.

# Get out.

Talk to the other remaining residents in your apartment building and figure out how to escape. Maybe, just maybe you'll be able to outrun the clock. For that to be a possibility, you will have to find time in those who are willing to give.

My role: Design, 3D Art, Texture painting, Programming
